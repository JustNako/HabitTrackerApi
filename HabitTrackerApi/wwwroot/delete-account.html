Delete your account

If you want to leave Flow, you can delete all your data permanently. We don't keep hidden copies.

This is permanent. Once you delete your account, all habits, streaks, logs, and personal data are gone. We cannot restore them.

Option 1: Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Flow on your device.
  2. Go to Profile Settings.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: Email us

If you can't access the app anymore, email codexsstudio@gmail.com with:

  • The email on your Flow account.
  • Your username.
  • A note saying you want your account deleted.

We'll verify your identity before processing it. Manual requests take up to 7 business days.

Guilt trips
The problem

Most habit apps are designed for robots

They assume you wake up at 6 AM, never get sick, and have the willpower of a monk. Real life is messier than that.

The guilt machine

Miss one day and your streak resets. Miss two and the app sends passive-aggressive notifications. By day three you've uninstalled it.

Too many options

You came to drink more water and now you're configuring a twelve-step morning routine with color-coded categories and weekly review sessions.

Generic advice

"Drink 8 glasses of water!" Thanks. You could also tell me to breathe. The advice is the same whether you're a student or a night-shift nurse.

Dashboard overload

Six charts, four progress rings, and a heatmap that looks like a weather radar. You just wanted to know if you stretched today.

What Flow does differently

Simple tools that stay out of your way

We kept the stuff that works and threw out the stuff that just looks good in screenshots.

Custom routine generator New

Tell the app what your day looks like and what you want to work on. It puts together a schedule that fits around your actual commitments, not some idealized version of your life. Works for night shifts, students, parents, and people who just want to do three things consistently.

Starter templates New

84 routines built by people who actually do them. Use one as a starting point and adjust it until it feels right.

Streaks

Streaks are annoying, but they work. That number going up is sometimes the only reason you do the dishes at 10 PM.

Pattern analyzer Pro

Looks at your history and shows you when you usually drop off. If you always skip Thursday gym sessions, it tells you before it happens again.

Friends and competition

Add friends, compare streaks, and keep each other honest. It works the same way gym buddies work: you show up because someone will notice if you don't. No leaderboards, no public shaming, just a quiet awareness that your friend is watching.

Focus timer New

Track how many minutes you spend on each habit. Small blocks of time add up faster than you think.

History Pro

Weekly and monthly charts. Clean, simple, no weather-radar heatmaps. Just your completion rate and your best days.

Reminders New

Notifications that fire when you have time, not in the middle of a meeting. Check off habits from the lock screen.

Routine generator

Describe your day, get a routine back

You type in something like "I work nights and have two kids" and it builds a routine around that. You can tweak it after, swap things out, change the times. It takes about 10 seconds.

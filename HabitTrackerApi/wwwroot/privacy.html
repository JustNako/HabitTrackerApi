Privacy policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

We built Flow to help you track habits, not to collect your data. This page explains what we store and why. We tried to keep it short.

What we collect

Only what we need to run the app:

  • Your email and encrypted password.
  • Habits you create, logs, streaks, focus times.
  • Friend connections if you add any.
  • Push notification tokens for reminders.
  • Subscription status if you buy Pro.

What we do with it

We use your data to sync habits across devices, send reminders, and show you your progress. We don't sell it, we don't share it with advertisers, and we don't do anything clever with it behind the scenes.

Security

Passwords are encrypted. All connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow standard practices. If anything ever leaks, we'll tell you immediately.

Third-party services

  • Firebase handles push notifications and account sync.
  • Groq AI processes routine generation requests. We only send anonymized habit descriptions to Groq. Your email, name, and personal details never leave our servers.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from the app settings. Once you do, everything is wiped from our databases. No hidden backups, no retention period.
